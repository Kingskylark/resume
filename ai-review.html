AI review

I read AI-generated responses the way a careful reviewer does. Is it true, did it follow the instruction, does the reasoning hold up, and is it safe to send. Then I write down why.

What I check

Accuracy
Facts, claims and invented details. I verify before I rate.
Reasoning and clarity
Whether the answer works step by step and whether a user would understand it.
Instruction compliance
Whether the response did what was asked, including the small constraints.
Safety and context
Unsafe content, bias, and answers that miss the context or culture of the question.
Formatting
Errors in structure and presentation that guidelines require to be right.
